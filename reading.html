Reading notes

  • Primary science deposits Start with the Figshare short version (DOI above), then the Long Form for the fuller anomaly survey and prediction list: OU / TU / OUEH, External Gravitational Forces, CMB as filtered field, JWST-era high-z structure, and proposed observational consequences.
  • Anisotropy context already cited in that deposit Secrest, N. J., et al. (2021). “A Test of the Cosmological Principle with Quasars.” ApJL 908:L51. https://doi.org/10.3847/2041-8213/abdd40. Motivating literature for anisotropy — not an EGC proof claim.
  • Draft empirical outline (this site) CMB temperature / anisotropy ↔ directional redshift residuals — Planck + Pantheon+ / DESI-style residuals; null hypothesis and falsification stated; no finished result claimed.
  • Roadmap datasets (summary) Planck; Pantheon+ / SN Ia; SDSS & CatWISE-class quasars; DESI & Euclid; JWST high-z samples; longer-term directional gravitational-wave backgrounds. See Predictions.

Informal drafts and chat logs are not part of the published claim set; prefer the Figshare deposits and the pages above.
